Adding it to a Shopify store

Copy your embed code

Log in to your GetShopReply dashboard and copy the code for your store.

Open your theme code

In Shopify admin go to Online Store, Themes, then the ⋯ menu, then Edit code. Open layout/theme.liquid.

Paste and save

Keep the supplied defer attribute. Paste the code just above the closing </body> tag and click Save. Open your storefront, then click the chat button to open the widget.

Duplicate your theme before editing and preview the change. If your store uses a custom Content Security Policy, allow GetShopReply scripts, styles and connections. If you switch themes later, paste the code into the new theme too. Not comfortable editing code? Email us and we’ll walk you through it.

What it does, and what it doesn’t

It handles

  • Shipping times, costs and countries
  • Return and refund policy questions
  • Anything your FAQs already answer
  • Capturing emails when it can’t answer

It doesn’t

  • Look up or change orders
  • Issue refunds in your store
  • Send email for you (you reply yourself)
  • Guarantee every answer. AI can make mistakes, so review your store info regularly.

Common questions

What happens when it doesn’t know the answer?

It says so and asks for the visitor’s email. The question appears in your dashboard for you to follow up.

Can it tell customers where their order is?

No. It doesn’t connect to order systems, so it answers policy and FAQ questions only. For “where’s my order?”, add your shipping times and tracking instructions to your FAQs, and it will point customers to them.

Will it make up answers?

It answers from the information you enter. AI can still make mistakes, so review your store info regularly and check the questions it couldn’t answer in your dashboard.

Does it work outside Shopify?

The widget is an embed code, so it can go on any storefront where you can edit the site code.

What happens when I use all my replies?

Visitors can still leave their question and email for you to follow up. Your allowance resets on the first day of each UTC month.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancelling stops future renewals, and you normally keep access until the paid period ends. See the refund policy for details.

Do I need to be technical to install it?

You paste one embed code into your storefront theme. Ask us if you get stuck at support@getshopreply.com.
